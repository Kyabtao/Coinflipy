💎 VIP Tiers (8) — monthly wagered thresholds & rakeback

Wagered counters reset on the 1st of each month UTC. Unclaimed rakeback is never lost. Exclusive cosmetics and free emoji rewards are permanent; discounts, queue priority, support and access benefits require the current monthly tier. Rates are bounded 0–20%.

⭐ Level Reward Manager

XP curve: level = floor(sqrt(XP/60)) + 1. BONUS rewards now run through level 50; milestone levels 30/40/50 also grant rare, epic and legendary cosmetics.
