COMMERCIAL ANALYTICS

Revenue Dashboard

Total staked volume, net platform profit, daily and weekly trends, and exportable transaction logs — every figure is derived from integer-subunit money math.

🧮 Ledger reconciliation

Net platform profit = Gross revenue − Promotional cost − Comps − Rakeback paid − Referral payouts. Gross revenue = Coin Toss fees + P2P Games fees + Series Cup rakes + Tournament rakes + Shop & commerce + Transfer fees + Auction fees. Top-ups are funding (cash-in) and withdrawals are cash-out — neither is ever counted as revenue.

💎 VIP Tiers (8) — monthly wagered thresholds & rakeback

Wagered counters reset on the 1st of each month UTC. Unclaimed rakeback is never lost. Exclusive cosmetics and free emoji rewards are permanent; discounts, queue priority, support and access benefits require the current monthly tier. Rates are bounded 0–20%.

⭐ Level Reward Manager

XP curve: level = floor(sqrt(XP/60)) + 1. BONUS rewards now run through level 50; milestone levels 30/40/50 also grant rare, epic and legendary cosmetics.
