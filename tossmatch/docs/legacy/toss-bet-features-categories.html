💰 Fees — admin per game type

Regular-bet fee: round(pot × feePct%), default 5% — the only game type it applies to. Series Cups and tournaments carry their own separate admin rates (5% / 10%). Nothing is ever double-charged. The house always keeps ≥1 coin per fee.

🎰 Jackpot — armed, percentage-paid

Every game adds max(0, min(fee−1, max(floor, 10%×fee))). Once the pool reaches the arming threshold (default 50), a 00 hash (1/256) pays payPct% of the pool (default 50%) — the remainder rolls over. Never from winnings.

💎 VIP — 6 monthly tiers

Bronze 0+ · Silver 1k+ (5%) · Gold 5k+ (10%) · Platinum 15k+ (15%) · Diamond 40k+ (18%) · Legend 100k+ (20%). Resets at month end; unclaimed rakeback survives; admin-controlled within 0–20%.

👠 Wallet & stakes

MAIN / REFERRAL / BONUS / RAKEBACK / BETTED / BANK segments. Transfers: main&to;main only (min 10, 500/day). Any stake: non-main ≤ 10–20%, rest from main; winnings → 100% main.

🔑 Fairness — one proof

combined = BigInt(hashA) + BigInt(hashB) + BigInt(SHA256(seed)). Commit before the flip, seed revealed after. Same proof covers the jackpot trigger. Copy-to-clipboard on every proof.

🕐 Seasons & real-player policy

Seasons auto-end monthly at 20:00 UTC (500/250 + title). ×2 room (10-min TTL) and tournaments (creator-only cancel) are real-players-only. Light/dark theme. Auto-rebet has a −200 session stop.

🏗️ Foundation systems

The core every feature runs on: matching, escrow, fairness, fees, fee-funded jackpot, ledger. · 7 features

⚡

P2P auto-match queue

F1

Every posted bet joins a FIFO queue. The engine instantly pairs a new bet with the oldest waiting bet from a different owner that has the exact same amount and the opposite side — matching fires in under a second, publishes the fairness commitments and auto-flips. If nothing matches, the bet waits visibly (queue position, amount, side, wait timer) and can be cancelled for a full refund at any time. Manual “Take” buttons always remain available, and the admin can toggle auto-match off (manual only) or back on, which sweeps every compatible waiting pair and settles it in the background.

WhereTables tab
ParametersRules: exact amount equality · opposite side only · no self-match · one match per bet (UNIQUE game_id in production)
v1
🏦

Escrow wallets

F2

Stakes never sit in the betting wallet: the moment a bet is posted, the stake leaves the player’s wallet into escrow and appears in the ledger. Escrow is atomic with posting and with matching, so a bet can never exist unbacked. Cancelling an unmatched bet refunds the stake in full; cancelling a matched-but-unflipped game refunds both players. Settlement releases the full pot − fee to the winner in the same atomic step. Stakes are funded per the v4.5 rule: 80–90% must come from the MAIN balance, with at most 10–20% (admin cap) drawn from bonus/referral/rakeback together — and winnings always land back in main (feature 66).

WhereAll betting
Parametersmain ≥ 80–90% of stake · nonMain ≤ cap% · one transaction · CHECK (≥ 0)
v1
🔐

3-party merged-hash fairness

F3

The coin result mathematically requires all three parties. Each player’s secret seed is hashed into a frozen bet fingerprint (SHA-256 of seed:betId:amount:side) at post time. When the match forms, the system generates a 256-bit CSPRNG server seed and publishes only SHA-256(serverSeed). The result is the first byte of HMAC-SHA256(serverSeed, makerHash + takerHash : gameId) — even = HEADS, odd = TAILS. Because every party contributes entropy that is committed before the flip, no one (including the house) can see or steer the outcome in advance; changing any single input avalanches the hash and is instantly detectable.

WhereEvery flip
Parameterscombined = BigInt(hashA)+BigInt(hashB)+BigInt(SHA256(seed)) · final = SHA256(combined:gameId)
v2
🔍

Commit–reveal proofs

F4

After every flip the full proof panel reveals all inputs step by step: the committed hash, the revealed server seed, both preimages and fingerprints, the BigInt sum in hex, the final SHA-256, and the first byte’s parity mapping — so anyone can recompute the coin by hand. The same single proof also covers the jackpot trigger (byte prefix “00”), and a standalone verifier accepts any game’s four inputs to re-derive the result locally. In production the verifier is a public web page; player seeds should be hashed client-side and revealed only after settlement.

WhereGame modal · Verify tab
ParametersOne proof covers result + jackpot · verified vectors (SHA-256/HMAC) in Appendix B of the docs
v2
💰

House fee engine

F5

The admin-controlled core revenue line, applied ONLY to regular matched bets (v4.4 scope). A configurable percentage of every pot (default 5%, range 0–25%) is deducted at settlement into segregated house revenue. Series Cups and tournaments never pay this fee — they carry their own separate admin rates (features 4 and 56) — and house games are RTP-priced, so no bet is ever double-charged. The rate is snapshotted per game at match time, mid-session changes never touch running games, and every change is audited. At defaults a 100-vs-100 game: pot 200 → fee 10 → winner 190, with the jackpot contribution coming out of that fee (F6).

WhereAdmin → rates
Parametersfee = round(pot × feePct%) · default 5% · regular bets only (v4.4) · funds the 💰 pool
v1
🎰

Hash-triggered jackpot

F6

A growing prize pool funded from the house fee of regular bets: each game contributes min(fee, max(1 coin, round(10% × fee))) — a 1-coin floor for small games, capped at the fee, never deducted from the winner’s payout. From v5.3 the jackpot is ARMED and percentage-paid: the trigger only fires once the pool reaches the admin-set arming threshold (default 50 coins) — below it a “00” byte hits for nothing and the pool keeps building. A hit pays the winner a configurable percentage (default 50%, floor 1%) while the remainder rolls over. The house always keeps at least 1 coin of every fee (v5.3.2), so micro-stakes no longer produce zero-margin games. The trigger rides the same merged hash (byte “00”, 1 in 256) and cannot be aimed at or read in advance; long-run, the funding is still returned 100% to players.

WhereHeader meter · every game
Parametersfund = min(fee, max(1, 10% × fee)) · armed when pool ≥ min (def 50) · pays payPct% of pool (def 50), remainder rolls over (v5.3)
v3
🧾

Conservation ledger

F7

Every single coin movement — escrow, refund, payout, fee, rake, jackpot, premium, claim, rakeback, shop, vault, comp, promo, reward — is a ledger entry with a running balance. The conservation invariant asserts at every instant that wallets + vaults + all escrow (queue, games, cups, tournaments, ladders) + house accounts (capital, fees, rakes, shop) + jackpot pool + rakeback liability equals every coin ever created. The automated test suite validates it across all 15 buckets on every code path; in production it becomes a nightly reconciliation job where any drift is a P0 incident.

WhereStats · Admin P&L
ParametersΣ accounts = Σ deposits + taps · tested on every path
v2

Segmented wallet

64

The wallet splits into labelled balances with different rules: MAIN holds deposits, top-ups, transfers in and 100% of winnings — the only transferable and withdrawable balance; REFERRAL holds referral-program credits; BONUS holds faucet, quest, achievement, login-streak, level and season rewards plus promos; RAKEBACK holds VIP claims; and BETTED shows stakes currently locked in open bets, cups, tournaments and ladders. Stake funding cap (10–20%)

66

On any bet, the non-main balances together may fund at most 10–20% of the stake (admin cap, default 20%) — the remaining 80–90% must always come from main, and the bet is rejected if main cannot cover it. Draw order for the capped slice: bonus first, then referral, then rakeback. All winnings — game payouts, jackpots, tournament prizes — are credited 100% to main. Example at the 20% cap: a 100-coin stake can use up to 20 bonus/referral/rakeback coins with ≥80 from main, and a 190-coin win lands entirely in main. This is the core bonus-abuse control: promotional money can sweeten stakes but never drive them.
