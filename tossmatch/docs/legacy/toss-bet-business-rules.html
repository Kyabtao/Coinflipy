2 🎰 Jackpot — Complete Rules

2.1 Funding

contribution = max(0, min(fee - 1, max(jackpotMin, round(fee * jackpotFeePct / 100))))

House always keeps ≥1 coin per fee. Stake 10 → fee 1 → pool gets 0, house keeps 1.

2.2 Arming threshold

Pool must reach minTrigger (default 50, admin 0–100,000). Below it: even a "00" byte pays nothing.

2.3 Trigger & payout

Trigger: final hash starts with byte "00" (1/256). Payout: payPct% of pool (default 50%, admin 1–100%). Remainder rolls over.
