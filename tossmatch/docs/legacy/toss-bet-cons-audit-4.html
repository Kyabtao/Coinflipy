🟠 B. Spec Design Issues (5 concerns)

#IssueDetailSuggestion
B1Double taxation5% deposit + 5% withdraw = 9.75% round-trip loss. Deposit 1000 → withdraw 902.50Cap total round-trip at 5% (e.g., 2.5% each) · or tax only withdrawals · or free deposits
B2Transfer fee negligibleMax 10 coins/day (2% of 500 cap) — barely worth the UI frictionRaise transfer cap or remove the fee (use it as a retention feature instead)
B3VIP tier gaps too large60k→80k→100k jumps are inaccessible to casual playersCompress top tiers (40k/60k/80k) or add intermediate milestones
B4Mythic items too expensive800–1500 coins; at 50/game, needs ~15,000 monthly wagered for one itemReduce Mythic range to 400–800 · or make them event rewards instead of purchasable
B5Quest window excludes IST players20:00 UTC = 1:30 AM IST — late-night Indian players miss quests entirelyExtend to 22:00 UTC (2:30 AM IST) · or use IST-based window · or run two windows

🟠 C. Economic Concerns (3 issues)

#IssueMathSuggestion
C1Total fee burden ~15.5% per cycleDeposit tax 5% + game fee 5% + withdraw tax 5% = 15%+ friction on a full deposit→play→withdraw cycleTarget 8–10% total; reduce deposit tax to 0% (tax only on withdrawals)
C2VIP exclusives = perceived pay-to-winLegend-tier players with Mythic skins + animated frames create visible hierarchyMake Mythic items earnable through achievements, not just wagering
C3Sound packs useless with default OFFPlayer buys a sound pack but hears nothing until manually enabling soundsAuto-enable sounds when a sound pack is purchased · or play a preview on purchase

🟠 D. Responsible Gaming Concern (1)

IssueDetail
Cosmetics discount tied to wageringVIP discount (10–30% off) incentivizes higher wagering volume to save on cosmetics. This is standard industry practice but borders on encouraging problem gambling. Consider: cap the discount at 20%, apply it to all players equally at certain levels, or decouple it from VIP entirely.
