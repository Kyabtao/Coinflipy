⚠️ 0. Legal notice — read first

A coin toss is a game of chance. Operating real-money games of chance is prohibited in most of India — Public Gambling Act 1867, state gaming laws, and the 2025 IT Amendment Rules restricting real-money online games. Delhi is not a licensed exception.

This document and the demo use play coins only — legal to run and share.

Any real-money operation must go through a licensed entity in a jurisdiction where chance-based gaming is legal (see §12), with legal counsel, KYC/AML and certified randomness in place before accepting a single rupee. Jackpots, VIP programs and promotions each add regulatory sensitivity — counsel required on every monetization layer.

By design, no plain result is ever stored before the flip — storing readable outcomes in advance is rigging, i.e. fraud against players.

1. System overview

TossMatch is a peer-to-peer matching system. The house never bets against the player — it matches two opposing players, holds both stakes in escrow, takes a configurable fee from the pot, and pays the entire remainder to the winner. v4 layers a full operator economy on top: fee engine, VIP rakeback, Series Cup rake, house games, cosmetics and controlled promotions — see §14 for how these combine into sustainable profit.

Design principles

  • The house is a matchmaker, not a counterparty. No game-risk; revenue = fee + cup rake + tournament rake + shop + house games (§14).
  • Escrow-first. Coins leave the wallet the moment a bet is posted.
  • Deterministic fairness. One hash decides coin and jackpot — nothing to rig (§7).
  • Every coin accountable. The conservation invariant spans wallets, vaults, escrow, cups, fees, jackpot pool and rakeback liability (§5).

3. End-to-end game flow

Step 1 — Player A posts a bet

  • Amount a, side, secret seed → frozen fingerprint betHashA = SHA-256(seedA:betId:a:sideA).
  • Optional taunt (≤24 chars); optional private flag (manual match only).
  • balanceA −= a; ledger entries; bet joins the FIFO queue.

Step 2 — Match (instant)

Player B posts the same amount on the opposite side → auto-match with the oldest eligible bet. Commitments published: commit = SHA-256(serverSeed), betHashA, betHashB.

Step 3 — The flip

combined  = BigInt(hashA) + BigInt(hashB) + BigInt(SHA256(serverSeed))
firstByte = merged[0..1] as hex → 0..255
result    = even → HEADS · odd → TAILS

Step 4 — Settlement (v4 economy)

pot         = 2·a
fee         = round(pot × feePct/100)          → house revenue
jackpotFund = min(round(pot × rakePct/100), fees) → jackpot pool (from fee revenue)
payout      = pot − fee                        → winner (full remainder)

JACKPOT     : merged starts with "00" (1/256) → winner takes the pool
VIP         : each player accrues tier% × ½·fee as claimable rakeback
PROGRESSION : XP += a both sides · quests · achievements · streaks · feed

Cancellation paths

  • Queued bet cancelled → stake refunded in full.
  • Matched game cancelled before flip → both stakes refunded.
  • Series Cup cancelled → both entries refunded.

Conservation invariant (must hold at every instant)

Σ(wallets) + Σ(vaults) + Σ(queue escrow + premiums) + Σ(ready pots) + cupEscrow + house fees/rakes/shop + jackpot.pool + Σ(rakebackPending) = Σ(deposits + bonuses + rewards + promos)
Every layer is a closed loop. The automated test suite asserts this across all accounts on every path.

6. Matching engine + auto-match queue

Core rules

  1. Amount equality — matchable only at the exact same amount.
  2. Opposite side only — taker receives the opposite side automatically.
  3. No self-match.
  4. One-to-one — each bet matches once (UNIQUE game_id backstop in production).
  5. Escrow precondition — escrow is atomic with posting/matching.
  6. Auto-match ON by default; private bets (🔒) are excluded from auto-match and the sweep — manual take only.

⚡ Auto-match queue

on bet_posted(b):
    if b.private: enqueue(b)                            # manual only
    m = first open non-private bet where (owner ≠ b.owner) AND (amount = b.amount) AND (side = opposite(b.side))
    if m exists: pair → commitments → auto-flip + settle
    else: enqueue(b)
  • Admin toggle & sweep — switching ON sweeps compatible public pairs and settles them in the background.
  • Production concurrency — queue in the DB; matching is one transaction: SELECT … FOR UPDATE / SKIP LOCKED, atomic OPEN → MATCHED, UNIQUE(bets.game_id) backstop.
Automated tests (all PASS): instant pairing · FIFO oldest-first · no self-match · private excluded from auto · sweep on toggle · manual take alongside auto · queue cancel refunds (stake + premium) · conservation across all accounts.

6A. 💰 Jackpot engine

A growing prize pool funded from house-fee revenue on every pot and won through the same provably-fair merged hash that decides the coin — there is no separate draw to manipulate.

ParameterDefaultDescription
contributionmin(fee, max(1, 10% × fee))1-coin floor for small games · paid out of the house fee at settlement — never deducted from the winner (v4.4)
Triggermerged[0..1] == "00"First byte zero → P = 1/256 ≈ 0.39% per game
Arming NEW v5.3pool ≥ minTrigger (admin, default 50)The trigger only fires once the pool has reached the threshold — below it the byte may hit but nothing pays, and the pool keeps building
Winnergame winnerReceives payPct% of the pool (admin, default 50%) — the remainder rolls over and keeps growing instead of resetting to zero
  • House keeps 0% of the rake — long-run the pool returns everything to players. It is a retention magnet, not a revenue line (§14).
  • Every pool movement is a ledger entry and joins the conservation invariant; the RNG-health monitor audits hit-rate vs 1/256.
  • Production: pool held as a segregated liability account; trigger odds published; retroactive changes = tampering.

7. Result engine — 3-party merged hash

The outcome and the jackpot trigger mathematically require all three fingerprints. No single party — including the house — can steer either.

#InputCreated whenPublic as
1betHashA = SHA-256(seedA:betId:a:sideA)bet postedhash only
2betHashB = SHA-256(seedB:gameId:a:sideB)match formshash only
3serverSeed (256-bit CSPRNG)match formscommit = SHA-256(serverSeed)
combined  = BigInt(hashA) + BigInt(hashB) + BigInt(SHA256(serverSeed))
result    = (firstByte even) ? HEADS : TAILS
jackpot   = (merged starts with "00")            # same hash — same proof

  1. Hash revealed serverSeed → equals published commit.
  2. Recompute both bet fingerprints from revealed preimages.
  3. Compute the merged HMAC → first-byte parity = result; prefix "00" = jackpot. One proof covers both.

10. Concurrency, integrity & security

  1. Double-take prevention — bet row locked FOR UPDATE; atomic OPEN → MATCHED; UNIQUE(bets.game_id) backstop.
  2. Double-spend prevention — balance + premium check and debits in one transaction; CHECK (wallet_balance ≥ 0).
  3. Idempotency — every money-moving endpoint takes an Idempotency-Key.
  4. Settlement atomicity — reveal, result, payout, fee, rake, jackpot, rebates, rakeback, XP, quests: one transaction.
  5. Fund solvency — rebate ≤ fund (CHECK (fund ≥ 0)); jackpot pool pays only what it holds; comps capped at house revenue.
  6. No plain outcomes ever stored; jackpot trigger derived from the same reveal.
  7. Bonus & promo abuse controls — server-side cooldowns, one-claim windows, device/IP velocity, rate limits (12 bets/min).
  8. Monitoring — conservation job across all accounts; jackpot hit-rate vs 1/256; coin-flip balance z-score; big-win review flags (payout ≥ 400).

Geo-block everywhere else — including all of India for real stakes. Jackpots (prize promotions), wager-insurance, VIP/rakeback programs and deposit promos are each regulated products in many markets — counsel on every monetization layer. This is engineering documentation, not legal advice.

  2. KYC/AML; payments via licensed PSPs; segregated player + liability accounts.

14.3 Retention flywheel (why VIP exists)

wager → XP → level → higher limits → more wager          (progression loop)
wager → VIP tier → rakeback → feels-free fees → stays   (loyalty pricing)
daily login + quests + cups → habit → session frequency (engagement taps)
jackpot meter → shared excitement → watch-the-pool       (event loop)

Rakeback is loyalty pricing, not a cost centre: returning 5–20% of fees to the top 20% of players is cheaper than replacing them with paid acquisition (CAC in real-money gaming is notoriously high). The P&L panel and simulator in the demo let an operator tune these numbers live.

14.4 Controlled taps & sinks (play-economy balancing)

The Economy Health gauge (Admin) tracks taps vs sinks so the play economy neither starves (players can't play) nor hyper-inflates (coins worthless → engagement dies). In a real-money build the same discipline applies to bonus abuse: every tap is server-capped, one-per-account, velocity-checked.

14.5 Longevity levers & guard-rails

  • Volume — auto-match keeps pairing latency < 1s; queues visible; instant mode for grinders.
  • Take-rate discipline — fee 4–6% sweet spot; the fee now also funds the jackpot pool, so price it with that cost in mind; never touch the fairness proof.
  • Promo ROI — first-deposit bonus tracked as explicit cost; target CAC payback < 30 days.
  • Reserves — 3 months opex float; liability accounts (jackpot/rakeback) never mixed with revenue.
  • Player protection = licence protection — loss limits, self-exclusion, reality checks keep the licence that keeps the business alive.
  • Monitoring — conservation reconciliation, RNG health, loss-ratio alerts, big-win review flags.

Appendix C — FAQ

Q: How does the system actually make money?
Six lines (§14): core fee (5% of pot), VIP net fee after rakeback, Series Cup rake (5% of cup pot), tournament rake (10%), house-games edge and the shop. The jackpot is fee-funded and returns 100% of its funding to players — it exists for retention.

Q: Isn't rakeback giving money away?
It's loyalty pricing: you return 5–20% of fees to your most active players because retained volume beats acquiring replacements. The P&L panel shows net fee after rakeback so the trade is always visible.

Q: What stops bonus abuse?
Every tap is capped: faucet cooldown, one promo per account, fixed quest/achievement rewards, 12 bets/min rate limit — and all of it is server-side in production.

Q: Why so many responsible-gaming features in a play demo?
Loss limits, self-exclusion and reality checks are licence conditions in every regulated market — building them from day one is what lets a real-money version survive.
